## A. Authenticity and disclosure
The app is an independent Catholic-themed **virtual devotional artwork** for TikTok LIVE, not a real parish, live Mass, priestly service, ecclesially endorsed ministry, or sacrament.
Suggested visible disclosure: **"Không gian nhà thờ Công giáo mô phỏng để suy niệm trực tuyến. Không phải Thánh lễ hay bí tích."**
Source for the boundary between internet and sacraments: Pontifical Council for Social Communications, *The Church and Internet* (2002), https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/pontifical_councils/pccs/documents/rc_pc_pccs_doc_20020228_church-internet_en.html . This is a design reference, not official endorsement.

## B. Catholic scene and symbolic free actions
- Architectural cues: **crucifix with Christ**, pews, stained glass, center aisle, reserved front altar/presbytery; all artwork must be original or properly licensed.
- Optional **Marian side devotional image/statue** only after Owner explicitly approves it. Do not assume approved just because the Catholic identity is approved.
- Viewer characters are ordinary visitors who enter, stand, sit or pause/kneel quietly in a pew or side devotional area. No priest, Eucharistic ceremony or consecration animations.
- `cầu nguyện` means a symbolic quiet visual; `thắp nến` lights a free **virtual votive candle** at a side stand; `amen` gives a simple acknowledgement. None promises a spiritual outcome.
- Keep the central sanctuary a **protected non-interactive polygon/mask**. No path, floating name, gift effect, visual flame, avatar or camera gimmick may overlap the crucifix/altar/presbytery.
- Quiet mode mutes TTS/ambient/SFX and reduces animated FX immediately.

## C. Explicit P0 prohibitions
- No simulated Mass, Communion, Eucharist, confession, absolution, sacraments or AI priest blessing; no named clergy impersonation, counterfeit parish affiliation or fabricated Marian/Jesus revelations.
- No gifts, coins or money to obtain grace, healing, forgiveness, priority prayer, more powerful candles or access to core religious actions.
- No gift explosions, animals, jokes, dances, currency icons, commercial graphics or loud SFX on sacred artwork.
- No public reading of raw user petitions, health issues, names of deceased or other sensitive prayer text. Use pre-approved short templates instead.
- No unlicensed music/hymns, devotional recordings, church photos, art or scripture translations.
